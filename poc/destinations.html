Destinations

We connect travellers to cities and towns across Kenya. Here are the destinations we serve.

Nairobi

The capital city — Kenya's vibrant hub of business, culture, and transport.

Mombasa

Coastal gem on the Indian Ocean, famous for its beaches and historic Old Town.

Kisumu

Lakeside city on the shores of Lake Victoria, rich in culture and natural beauty.

Eldoret

Highland city known for its elite athletes, cool climate, and growing economy.

Nakuru

Home to the flamingo-fringed Lake Nakuru National Park and a key Rift Valley stop.

Busia

Border town connecting Kenya to Uganda, a lively trade and travel gateway.

Bumala

A growing town in Busia County, known for its agricultural activity.

Kangemi

Neighbourhood on the edge of Nairobi, a key stop along the western corridor.

Luanda

A busy market town in Vihiga County, buzzing with local commerce and energy.

Maseno

University town straddling the equator, known for Maseno University.

Ugunja

A serene rural town in Siaya County, surrounded by rolling green hills.

Uthiru

A bustling suburb along Nairobi's western edge, popular with students and commuters.
